✨ Faith Reflection & Study

Today's Theme: Stewards of Creation

Historical Context:

Lehi's family had left Jerusalem with only what they could carry. God commanded them to return to Jerusalem - a dangerous journey - to obtain the brass plates (scripture records) from Laban, a powerful military leader.

This was a test of faith. Nephi's older brothers, Laman and Lemuel, murmured and complained. But Nephi's response reveals a fundamental principle of discipleship.

Deep Dive Questions:

  • Notice what Nephi DIDN'T say: He didn't say "I know HOW to get the plates." He said "I will go and do." Why is this significant?
  • The promise: "The Lord giveth no commandments... save he shall prepare a way." What does this teach about timing? About God's role vs. our role?
  • Modern application: When have you been asked to do something hard without knowing exactly how it would work out? How did it go?

Elder Neal A. Maxwell taught that "faith precedes the miracle." Write about a time when you had to act in faith before seeing how things would work out. Or, write about a challenge you're facing now where you need to trust that "God will prepare a way."

This week, when faced with something difficult (a hard assignment, a social situation, a family task), try responding with "I will go and do" before you know exactly how you'll succeed. Document what happens.

📚 Literature & Analysis

Literary Skill Focus: Theme vs. Main Idea

Understanding the Difference:

  • Main Idea: What the text is about (summary)
  • Theme: The deeper message or life lesson (universal truth)

Example:

In "The Tortoise and the Hare," the main idea is that a slow tortoise races a fast hare and wins. The theme is "slow and steady wins the race" or "overconfidence leads to failure."

Practice Passage:

Marcus had studied all week for the math test. Every night he reviewed his notes and did practice problems. His friend Jake bragged that he didn't need to study because math was "easy" for him. On test day, Marcus felt prepared. When the results came back, Marcus earned a 95%. Jake got a 72%. "I guess I should have studied," Jake admitted.

Analysis Questions:

  • What is the main idea of this passage?
  • What is the theme? (What universal truth does it teach?)
  • What evidence from the text supports the theme?

🌍 History & Current Events

March 3rd is UN World Wildlife Day! This day celebrates the diversity of wild animals and plants and raises awareness about their conservation.

Key Historical Milestone: CITES (1973)

On March 3, 1973, the Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species of Wild Fauna and Flora (CITES) was adopted. This landmark international agreement ensures that international trade in specimens of wild animals and plants does not threaten their survival. Today, CITES protects over 35,000 species.

The 2026 Theme: "Medicinal and Aromatic Plants: Conserving Health, Heritage, and Livelihoods."

  • Many modern medicines are derived from wild plants.
  • Habitat loss and climate change are putting these vital resources at risk.
  • How can we balance our need for resources with the need for conservation?
